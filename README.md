# 📚 Collex: Your Campus Marketplace

Welcome to **Collex**, the one-stop marketplace designed exclusively for students! This low-cost, high-demand platform empowers verified students to buy, sell, and rent campus essentials—from textbooks to electronics and more.

Built with a focus on **trust**, **speed**, and **seamless logistics**, Collex aims to be the go-to solution for all your on-campus needs.

---

## 🎯 Vision & Differentiators

Our core philosophy is to build a platform that is **trusted**, **fast**, and **community-focused**.

- **Campus-Only, Verified**  
  Access is gated to verified college students, ensuring a safe and trustworthy environment.

- **Fast & Intuitive Listing**  
  Create listings in under a minute with title, photos, price, and description.

- **Lightweight Logistics**  
  Optional campus delivery and pre-defined meet-up points simplify transactions.

---

## 🚀 Key Features (MVP)

- **Authentication & Verification**  
  Secure sign-in via email/OTP, with college domain verification or manual ID approval.

- **Listings**  
  Create, view, and manage product listings with photos, details, and status updates (Active, Reserved, Sold).

- **Search & Discovery**  
  Find items easily using keyword search, category filters, and sorting options.

- **In-App Chat**  
  Real-time, secure messaging between buyers and sellers.

- **Transactions**  
  Complete deals using COD or UPI, with post-transaction ratings.

- **Admin Panel**  
  Admin tools for moderating listings and maintaining community safety.
